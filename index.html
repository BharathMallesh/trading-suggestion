Call / Put payoff odds VOLATILITY-BASED · RESEARCH

The question an option buyer faces: what is the chance this option ends above breakeven at expiry? Answered from the volatility forecast, not from a direction call (the app has no direction skill). Audit: when it says 80% it happened ~79% of the time (within ~1.5 points). The volatility input is the HAR forecast, which beat simply using last year's volatility on 14 years of out-of-sample data (t −2.6) — a small but real edge (~0.5% better scores). Treat the odds as honest, not as a trading signal. Compares the forecast with what the option's own price implies, and its fair value at forecast volatility with the premium. Leave premium blank to price it from implied vol (India VIX for NIFTY, IV you enter, or Groww's chain). Not a recommendation.

Big-move odds SIZE, NOT DIRECTION · RESEARCH

The chance the next session or week moves more than ±k%, up or down. This part is predictable: volatility clusters, and results days are known. Odds come from the HAR volatility forecast plus the stock's own past fat-tailed moves, and are compared with how often it happened over the past year. The replay shows whether the odds beat that on history, including stocks never used for fitting. Not a direction call; not a recommendation.

Options positioning PCR · OI · SKEW → NIFTY · EVIDENCE TEST

Do the signals traders watch in NIFTY options (put-call ratio, open-interest build-up, put skew) predict NIFTY's next day or week? Tested on every session since 2019 from NSE's daily F&O files, with fixed rules, both halves of the period, and a stricter bar because 12 tests are run. Research only.

Selling volatility NIFTY WEEKLY OPTIONS · REPLAY · PAPER ONLY

Option prices usually assume bigger moves than actually happen, so sellers of options collect a premium on average, and occasionally take a large loss. This replays three fixed strategies on 7+ years of NIFTY weekly options after Indian charges and slippage: first priced from India VIX, then on actual NSE closing prices. The real-price result decides. Not a recommendation: short options can lose many weeks of profit in one week.

Volatility check OPTIONS · RESEARCH

Compares the volatility options are pricing (implied) with a forecast from recent price behaviour. NIFTY uses India VIX automatically; for a stock, enter the option's IV % from your broker — or set GROWW_ACCESS_TOKEN on the server and it is read from Groww's option chain (ATM IV, put/call OI ratio). Volatility is where this app has shown measurable skill — direction is not. Factual comparison, not a trade suggestion.

Paper portfolio SIMULATED · PERSISTENT

A simulated delivery account that remembers its positions between sessions. Apply today's signals buys on daily LONG signals (confidence, position and risk limits from Settings) and exits held names on FLAT signals after the minimum hold; stops and targets are checked against the daily bars since entry. Long-only (retail can't hold cash-market shorts overnight), Indian charges applied. No orders are ever sent. Not investment advice.

Model evaluation REPLAY · CALIBRATION

Replays past bars: at each bar the Call / Put / Sideways model makes its prediction, which is scored against what happened next, side by side with simple baselines. Brier: lower is better (33/33/33 scores 0.667). Skill: improvement over just quoting historical base rates; above 0 means real information. Fit & use stores probabilities fitted to the data; live Call/Put uses them only if they beat the formula on bars they weren't fitted on. Research only — past skill is no promise of future accuracy.
